About the program

Biology is a story. Read it that way.

Textbooks give you facts. Bibliophage gives you the logic underneath the facts — the experiments, the arguments, the elegant ideas that took decades to build. You don't memorise here; you understand.

This summer, we read a landmark text in molecular biology together — a book celebrated for making one of biology's most intricate mechanisms genuinely compelling. It is the kind of writing that makes you feel like you are inside the science as it happened, not reading about it from a safe distance.

What makes this program different: we read across silos. Genetics, biochemistry, physics, and even a little philosophy show up in the same conversation. That's how real biology works — and that's how we read it.

You will leave not just knowing more, but thinking differently about what it means to understand something in science.

"Deeper than jargon. Deeper than facts. Into the story of how we know what we know."

This program is for students curious enough to ask why and patient enough to sit with complexity until it starts to make sense.

How it works

Two modes of reading together

Asynchronous

Social reading, online

Each week you read assigned chapters on a shared social learning platform — where your annotations, questions, and reactions appear inline in the text, visible to the whole group. You don't just read; you read together, even when apart.

Annotations are due 48 hours before the weekly session. The most generative threads become the starting point for live discussion.

Open all week · Annotations due 48 hrs before Zoom
Synchronous

Weekly live discussion

Every Saturday evening (IST) we meet on Zoom for 60 minutes of real conversation — not a lecture, not a quiz. We talk about what was interesting, what felt vague, and what still doesn't quite make sense.

Every voice matters. This is a reading group, not a classroom. Confusion is welcome here.

Saturdays · 7 PM IST · 60 minutes · Zoom
